2. Your notes and local data

My Notes stores note titles and contents, checklists, categories, favorites, sorting information, archived and trashed notes, and settings primarily on your device. The app does not require its own account or operate its own note-syncing server.

You can choose to export/import a local JSON backup or share notes via Android. Your chosen destination may process the exported information. Android system backup options may apply depending on your device settings.

3. Advertising with Google AdMob

The free version may display ads through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and transmit:

  • Device identifiers such as advertising ID and app-set ID;
  • IP addresses, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • Product interactions, such as app launches and ad interactions;
  • Diagnostic and performance information.

Google documents advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as purposes and states that SDK transmissions are encrypted using TLS. See Google's SDK data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Consent and privacy choices

According to the reported v2.1 implementation, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable regional advertising notices and consent choices in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. When available or required, you can revise those choices under App settings → Privacy → Manage advertising consent. Withdrawal applies to future processing; some technical data may still be processed when limited or non-personalized ads are shown.

Depending on the processing, the legal basis may include GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) for consent and Art. 6(1)(f) for certain legitimate security or operation purposes, alongside applicable device-access rules.

5. Google Play in-app purchases

The optional one-time remove_ads purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment processing; the app uses purchase state and the necessary product/purchase information to verify or restore the entitlement. We do not receive full bank or credit-card details through the app. Google's privacy policy applies.

6. Third-party services and sharing

We do not sell your written notes. Advertising and purchase services can transmit relevant technical data to Google. You control destinations when you explicitly export or share notes. External links are subject to the respective providers' privacy terms; cross-border processing may occur.

7. Retention and deletion

Notes and settings generally remain on your device until deleted. According to v2.1 functionality, deleted notes remain in trash for up to 30 days unless permanently deleted earlier. Exported backup files may require separate deletion. Third-party data retention follows Google's policies; uninstalling does not necessarily erase data already held by third parties.

8. Your privacy rights

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability and object to processing or withdraw consent. Contact flondy.dev@gmail.com. For data processed by Google, you may also contact Google. You may complain to a competent data protection authority.

9. This website

This is a static GitHub Pages website. Its own code does not embed analytics, advertising trackers, marketing cookies or externally hosted fonts. Hosting may process technical connection data such as IP address, access times and requested pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following a Google Play link leads to Google's own services.

10. Updates

This notice may be revised when the app, its services or applicable rules change.
